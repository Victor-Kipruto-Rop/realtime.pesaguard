# Production Readiness Skill

Before go-live verify requirements and ownership; authentication and tenant isolation; real provider/broker contracts; absence of fake success states; secrets, TLS, origin controls, limits and validation; health/readiness semantics; logs, metrics, alerts and runbooks; recovery and rollback; passing security, integration, regression and load tests; privacy/retention review; controlled rollout and accountable owner.

A scaffold, successful compilation or liveness check alone does not prove production readiness. List blockers explicitly.
